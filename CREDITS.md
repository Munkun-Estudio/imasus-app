# Origin, Authorship, and Contributions

IMASUS App is open-source software created and stewarded by Munkun SL. Opening
the source code does not erase the origin of the project or the distinct
contributions that made it possible. This document records that provenance.

## Origin and software authorship

Munkun SL conceived the original IMASUS project in 2023 and wrote the project
proposal. Munkun led the product and experience design and designed and
developed the software platform that became IMASUS App. Munkun SL remains the
author and steward of the reusable open-source application.

The principal creators are:

- **Munkun SL** — originating organisation, software author, and project
  steward;
- **Pablo Jimeno Pérez** — principal creator,
  [ORCID 0009-0002-0588-7715](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-0588-7715),
  affiliated with Munkun SL;
- **Guillermo Orduña** — principal creator,
  [ORCID 0009-0001-0114-6138](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-0114-6138),
  affiliated with Munkun SL.

## Research and content contributions

The installation-specific research and educational content in the IMASUS
profile includes distinct partner contributions:

- **Institute of Nanoscience and Materials of Aragón (INMA)** carried out the
  materials research;
- **Lottozero** carried out the research underpinning the training modules and
  challenges.

These contributions are credited separately from authorship of the shared
software platform. Content, media, names, logos, and trademarks retain their
stated licences and ownership and are not automatically covered by the MIT
License applied to the original software.
